# Research & Technical Writing

This collection translates my AI research and engineering work into practitioner-focused articles for research, architecture, and production audiences.

The writing is organized around three themes:

1. **Reliable Medical AI** — evaluation, calibration, uncertainty, explainability, human review, and research-to-deployment boundaries.
2. **Agentic AI & RAG Platforms** — governed tool use, retrieval, evaluation, observability, human approval, and enterprise architecture.
3. **Production AI Engineering** — MLOps, cloud AI platforms, CI/CD, data platforms, reproducibility, security, and responsible AI.
