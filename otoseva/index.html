MONAI-enabled digital health workflow

Safer first-pass ear screening, with uncertainty built in.

OtoSeva AI converts digital otoscope images into a quality-aware, calibrated and auditable screening workflow that helps clinics, schools, screening camps and tele-ENT programmes decide when to proceed, repeat capture or escalate for clinician review.

End-to-end MONAI workflow

1

Guided capture

Digital otoscope or smartphone-compatible capture with session ID, operator checklist and consent status.

2

Quality gate

Checks blur, glare, exposure, obstruction, field of view and visible tympanic-membrane coverage before inference.

3

MONAI processing

Versioned resize, normalization and reproducible transforms prepare images for consistent model inference.

4

AI + reliability

Four-class screening support with calibrated confidence, entropy-based uncertainty and accept/defer logic.

5

Clinician queue

Probabilities, referral priority, review notes, overrides and versioned audit trail support human decision-making.

6

Monitoring

Tracks image quality, deferral, clinician override, class-wise performance, calibration and device-specific drift.

Designed for affordable scale

Schools & communities

Structured screening and earlier referral for children and families who may not have immediate ENT access.

PHCs & clinics

Quality-controlled capture plus a standardized referral pathway for first-contact providers.

ENT & telemedicine

Prioritized review queues instead of unsorted cases, with uncertainty and audit information visible to the reviewer.

Commercial pathway: clinic SaaS + usage, school/camp screening packages, district pilots and API/white-label integration. Initial implementation target: 5,000–10,000 screenings during staged pilots, subject to partner approvals.
