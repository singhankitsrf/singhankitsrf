# OtoSeva AI

**MONAI-based human-supervised otoscopic screening and referral workflow**

Public product and prototype materials for OtoSeva AI.

- Product page: `otoseva/index.html`
- Interactive workflow demo: `otoseva/demo.html`

## Current evidence boundary
Internal retrospective technical validation supports feasibility of the governed screening and referral workflow. OtoSeva AI is not presented as an autonomous diagnostic system. External, device-diverse prospective validation and applicable regulatory assessment are planned before clinical deployment.
