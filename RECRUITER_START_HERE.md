## Engineering evidence standard

My portfolio intentionally separates:

1. **Architecture intent** — what a system is designed to do.
2. **Implemented artifacts** — source code, APIs, infrastructure definitions, tests and CI.
3. **Reproducible evaluation** — measured outputs with explicit dataset/configuration boundaries.
4. **Deployment evidence** — proof that a system or demo is actually running.
5. **Production/clinical claims** — made only when separately supported by real operational or validation evidence.

I do not present unexecuted cloud plans, synthetic benchmarks or research prototypes as production-scale or clinically validated results.
